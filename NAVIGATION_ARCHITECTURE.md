# Heritage Oven – Website Navigation & UX Architecture

This document outlines the strategic navigation and discovery architecture designed to provide a premium, frictionless experience for Heritage Oven customers.

---

## 1. Primary Navbar Structure (Desktop)
*Objective: Clean, luxury aesthetic with immediate access to high-conversion categories.*

*   **Left Section:**
    *   **Shop All** (Mega-menu trigger)
    *   **Cakes** (Direct link to high-margin signature collection)
    *   **Healthy** (Direct link to the growing Wellness segment)
*   **Center Section:**
    *   **[HERITAGE OVEN LOGO]** (Brand anchor)
*   **Right Section:**
    *   **Our Story** (Trust building)
    *   **Visit Us** (Local SEO/Physical store focus)
    *   **🔍 Search** | **👤 Account** | **🛒 Cart (Count)**

---

## 2. Mobile Navigation & UX
*Objective: Thumb-friendly, fast discovery, and "App-like" feel.*

*   **Header:** Hamburger Menu (Left) | Logo (Center) | Cart (Right)
*   **Sticky Category Pills (Below Hero):** 🎂 Cakes | 🥨 Snacks | 🥗 Healthy | 🎁 Gifts
*   **Bottom Navigation Bar (Fixed):**
    *   **🏠 Home**
    *   **✨ Discover** (Triggers the AI Recommendation Widget)
    *   **📦 My Orders**
    *   **📍 Visit**

---

## 3. Category & Smart Collection Hierarchy
*Objective: Logical grouping based on customer intent, not just product types.*

### **Tier 1: Celebrations (High AOV)**
*   **Signature Cakes** (Classic Pineapple, Black Forest, Rocky Road)
*   **Artisanal Pastries** (Biscoff Cheesecake, Belgium Devil)
*   **Party Essentials** (Balloons, Poppers, Banners)

### **Tier 2: The Daily Room (High Frequency)**
*   **Savory Bites** (Patties, Rolls, Kulchas)
*   **Tea-Time Essentials** (Biscuits, Traditional Namkeens)
*   **The Wellness Pantry** (Roasted Makhanas, Healthy Munchies)

### **Tier 3: Beverages & Desserts**
*   **Coolers & Refreshments** (Amul Lassis, Cold Drinks)
*   **Ice Cream Parlor** (Cones, Cups, Kulfis)

---

## 4. Homepage Discovery Flow (Strategic Ordering)
*Objective: Optimized for conversion and brand storytelling.*

1.  **Hero Section:** High-impact visual of a Signature Cake + "Order for Today" CTA.
2.  **Category Scroller:** Emotional icons (🎂, ☕, 🥗, 🎁).
3.  **The Heritage Favorites:** Best Seller carousel (Social Proof).
4.  **"Choice Assistant" (AI Widget):** Mid-page interaction to help undecided users.
5.  **Signature Collections:** Large, immersive cards for "Premium Cakes" and "Wellness Pantry".
6.  **Perfect Pairings:** Combo recommendations (Increased AOV).
7.  **Quality Promise:** Minimalist icons (100% Eggless | Baked Fresh | Premium Sourced).
8.  **Instagram Showcase:** Shoppable social proof.

---

## 5. Internal Linking & Discovery Logic
*Objective: Maximize page authority and guide user journey.*

*   **Product Page Cross-linking:**
    *   Cake Pages -> Link to **Party Essentials** ("Complete the party").
    *   Snack Pages -> Link to **Beverages** ("Perfect with a cold drink").
    *   Healthy Snack Pages -> Link to **Tea-Time Essentials** ("A better pairing for your chai").
*   **SEO Footnotes:**
    *   Deep-link to "Birthday Cakes Near Me" from the footer.
    *   Link "Best Bakery in Ghaziabad" to the About Us page.

## 7. AI-Search Readiness
*Objective: Structured for LLM discovery.*

*   **Semantic Breadcrumbs:** Home > Cakes > Signature > [Product Name]
*   **Schema.json Placement:** Integrated on every product page for "Price," "Availability," and "Local Store Info."
*   **Glossary Linking:** Link key terms (e.g., "100% Eggless") to a dedicated "Our Promise" page.
