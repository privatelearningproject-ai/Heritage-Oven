# Heritage Oven – Addictive Discovery-Commerce Architecture

This architecture transforms Heritage Oven from a static catalog into a high-engagement food experience, optimized for impulse buying, emotional cravings, and high average order value (AOV).

---

## 1. The "Impulse & Intent" Navigation Structure
*Moving away from "Product Type" to "Eating Occasion".*

*   **⚡ Quick Bites:** (Patties, Rolls, Sandwiches, Under ₹99)
*   **☕ Tea-Time Ritual:** (Biscuits, Namkeens, Traditional Snacks)
*   **🥗 Wellness Pantry:** (Makhanas, Diet Snacks, Roasted Superfoods)
*   **🎂 The Celebration Hub:** (Cakes, Party Supplies, Combos)
*   **🎁 Gifting Studio:** (Premium Hampers, Assorted Boxes)

---

## 2. New Homepage Discovery Flow (Addictive Scroll Strategy)

### **I. The "Now & Next" Hero (Dynamic)**
*   **09:00 - 12:00:** "Breakfast Bakery & Fresh Breads"
*   **12:00 - 16:00:** "Office Hunger Rescue: Fresh Rolls & Patties"
*   **16:00 - 19:00:** "The Tea-Time Ritual: Ghee Biscuits & Savories"
*   **19:00 - 00:00:** "Late Night Cravings: Pastries & Midnight Snacks"

### **II. ⚡ The ₹99 Impulse Shop (High-Velocity Row)**
*   **Concept:** A fast-moving, horizontal scroller of individual snacks and small treats under ₹99.
*   **Psychology:** Low friction, "add-to-cart" without thinking.
*   **Items:** Aloo Patty, Single Pastry Slices, Mini Makhana Packs, Cold Drinks.

### **III. 🏢 Office Hunger Rescue (The "Energy" Collection)**
*   **Concept:** Protein-rich, non-messy snacks for workplace eating.
*   **Combos:** Paneer Tikka Roll + Cold Coffee, Sandwich + Chips.
*   **Messaging:** "No more boring desk lunches."

### **IV. ☕ The 4PM Tea-Ritual (Tradition + Crunch)**
*   **Concept:** Cross-category pairing of sweet and salty.
*   **Mixed Collection:** Atta Patti Biscuits + Ratnam Mixture + Masala Chai (Home Pack).
*   **Visual:** Symmetrical stacks of biscuits next to bowls of namkeen.

### **V. 🎉 Build Your Party (The "Bundle" Engine)**
*   **Concept:** Step-by-step interactive builder.
*   **Step 1:** Pick a Cake.
*   **Step 2:** Pick Savory Snacks (Patties/Kulchas).
*   **Step 3:** Pick Decor (Balloons/Poppers).
*   **Benefit:** Increases AOV by 3x by solving the entire "hosting" problem.

### **VI. 🥗 The Guilt-Free Zone (Wellness Discovery)**
*   **Concept:** Clean aesthetic, focusing on "Roasted, not Fried."
*   **Items:** Full range of Farmley Makhanas, Diet Namkeens.
*   **Messaging:** "Snack like there's no tomorrow."

## 4. Mobile-First Discovery Behavior

*   **"Quick Add" Floating Action:** A (+) button on every product thumbnail that bypasses the product page for impulse items (Chips, Patties).
*   **"Shake for a Snack":** A playful interaction where shaking the phone gives a random ₹49-₹99 snack recommendation.
*   **"Frequently Bought with your Chai":** Dynamic cross-sell at the bottom of the cart showing biscuits and namkeens.

---

## 5. Visual Strategy for Collection Cards
*   **Density:** High density for "Impulse" sections (smaller cards, more items); Low density for "Celebrations" (large, immersive imagery).
*   **Micro-copy:** Instead of "Price," use "Only ₹99" or "Party Ready".
*   **Category Overlap:** Never show a section with just one product type. A "Celebration" card should show a cake *with* a party popper in the frame.

---

## 6. Product Density & Hierarchy
*   **Impulse (Top of Page):** 6-8 items in a fast scroller.
*   **Intent (Middle of Page):** 2-3 large "Experience" banners (Party, Office, Ritual).
*   **Exploration (Bottom of Page):** Grid of 12-16 varied products from all categories.
